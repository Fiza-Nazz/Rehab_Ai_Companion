Ji bhai, bilkul sun raha hoon! Aapka project ek bohot hi advanced aur life-changing healthcare platform hai jiska naam "AI Rehab Companion" hai.

Agar main simple alfazon mein bataun to: Ye ek Artificial Intelligence (AI) powered virtual doctor/assistant hai jo un patients ki madad karta hai jinki amputation (surgery ke zariye koi hissa katna) hui ho ya jo prosthetics (masnooi aaza) use kar rahe hon.

Yahan iski Top 5 Core Capabilities hain k ye karta kya hai:

1. 🏥 Daily Health Tracking (Check-ins)
Patient rozana app par aakar apna haal batata hai (Pain score, fatigue, mobility aur mood). App in saari details ko database mein safely record karti hai.

2. 🤖 AI-Generated Exercise Plans
Aam rehab apps mein static videos hoti hain, lekin aapki app mein Google Gemini AI integrate hai. AI patient ki aaj ki condition, surgery type, aur pichle dinon ka dard dekh kar uske liye Rozana ek naya, completely personalized aur safe exercise plan banata hai. Agar patient ko pain zyada hai, to AI automatically halki (low-impact) exercises dega.

3. 📈 Future Setback Forecasting (Bhavishya ki paishgoi)
Ye aapke project ka sabse advanced feature hai! Isme humne Facebook Prophet (Machine Learning) use kiya hai. Ye AI pichle kuch dinon ka data analyze karke pehle se bata deta hai k "Is patient ko agle 3 din mein dard barhne ka ya setback hone ka high risk hai", taake waqt se pehle action liya ja sake.

4. 🚨 Automated Smart Alerts (WhatsApp & Email)
Agar AI ko lagta hai k patient ki tabiyat kharab ho rahi hai ya wo exercise miss kar raha hai, to app khud-ba-khud Celery Background Workers k zariye Patient, Doctor, aur Caregiver ko WhatsApp aur Email par professional alerts bhejti hai.

5. 💬 24/7 AI Chatbot Support
App ke andar ek AI Chatbot mojood hai. Agar patient raat ko 2 baje pooche k "Mere prosthetic limb (masnooi bazu) mein chubhan ho rahi hai, main kya karun?" to AI bot uski medical profile dekh kar usay foran safe aur helpful advice dega.

💎 Design & UI
Aur ye sab kuch ek aam si boring app mein nahi hai, balkay iska Next.js Frontend bohot hi premium hai! Iske dashboard mein Three.js ki real 3D animations (DNA Helix, floating medical orbs, prosthetic arms) chal rahi hain jo isey bilkul ek futuristic medical software banati hain.

Short Summary: Aapka project ek AI-Driven Personal Physiotherapist aur Medical Monitor hai jo patient ki recovery ko fast, safe aur smart banata hai!
